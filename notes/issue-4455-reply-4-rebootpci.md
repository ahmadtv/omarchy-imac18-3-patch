@taprobane99 Thanks — and your `reboot=pci` was the clue. I tested it here as a control: the build from *before* any shutdown handling, warm-rebooted with `reboot=pci`, gives a straight Apple logo. So the hard PCI reset hides the woken tile, which is why you never see it. Two costs on this iMac18,3: a visibly longer black gap before the logo (the firmware re-inits more after a hard reset), and the ramoops region doesn't survive it, so shutdown/crash captures are lost. I'm keeping the driver-side handoff — straight logo at normal reboot speed — but that's the explanation for the difference between our machines, and anyone on `reboot=pci` doesn't need the handoff.
